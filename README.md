Shreedesh's Portfolio

A little bit of me, a little bit of code, and soo much of the things I LOOOVEE.

Welcome to my personal portfolio! This isn't just a generic website I made with my name on it, its a digital world built around the things I love, the place I come from, the things that shaped me and the ideas I brought to the world. 

Wondering what's inside?
- Minecraft-Inspired Design
Minecraft has been one of my favourite games since childhood, so I brought a whole lot of that nostalgia into my portfolio. This portfolio has Minecraft like fonts and Minecraft background track.

- A Touch of Space
I love space so I thought why not keep that in my portfolio. There's a moon and cosmic stars in the portfolio. The whole landing page is on space. Its cool so why not.

- A Tribute to Nepal
The temple is a nod to my country and its culture, a small detail that makes this project feel more personal.

- Arcade
The entire portfolio embraces an arcade inspired aesthetic, so that my portfolio just doesn't seem like the generic ones.

Built With

HTML5 - Was the foundation of the portfolio, giving structure to every element.
CSS3 - Made the styling and looks of the page with it
JavaScript - Most of the 3d works was done with it (using a little bit of help with Ai)

Simple technologies, combined to create something that feels uniquely mine.

The Idea Behind It

I wanted to create something that represents more than just my coding skills. Every element has a reason for being there, from the Minecraft nostalgia to the cosmic background and the Nepal inspired details.
